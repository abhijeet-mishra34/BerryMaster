## 1. Core Farming Mechanics

In PokéMMO, berry farming is a persistent, real-time economic engine. Unlike the handheld games where berry growth was tied to game-clock manipulation, PokéMMO runs on **real-world server time**:

- **Real-Time Progression**: Berries grow whether you are logged in or logged off.
- **Moisture Depletion**: Soil naturally dries out over time based on the berry's specific farming profile.
- **Yield Calculation**: Final harvest quantity (typically 4 to 12 berries per plant) depends directly on whether soil was kept adequately watered throughout all growth stages. If a plant stays dry for too long, its yield degrades to the minimum baseline.
- **Wilt / Rot Window**: Once ripe, every crop has an **8-hour harvest window**. If left unharvested after this 8-hour window expires, the crop withers and rots away into ash, yielding zero berries.

## 3. Soil Moisture & Watering Stages

When you examine a plot in-game, you will see one of four moisture descriptions:

1. **Very Soft / Dripping (Hydration: 100%)**:
   - Soil is freshly saturated. No watering needed.
2. **Soft / Moist (Hydration: ~50-75%)**:
   - Soil is healthy and hydrated. Berries are growing at normal speed.
3. **Dry (Hydration: ~25%)**:
   - Soil moisture is depleted. **This is your prompt to water!** Watering now returns the soil to 100%.
4. **Very Dry / Hard (Hydration: 0%)**:
   - Plants are starving for moisture. Extended time in this state reduces harvest yield penalties.

> [!TIP]
> **Can you over-water?** No! In PokéMMO, watering a plant that is already moist will simply reset the moisture timer back to 100%. You cannot drown or kill crops by watering too frequently.

## 6. Seed Extraction & Re-seeding Economy

Every planted berry plot requires **3 seeds** to germinate. When harvested, berries are processed in a Seed Extractor to produce raw seeds:

### Seed Extraction Yield Rates:
- **1x Harvested Berry** produces **1 to 2 Seeds** (random distribution, average ~1.45 seeds per berry).
- **Leppa Berry Recipe**:
  - Requires: `2x Very Plain Spicy Seeds` + `1x Very Plain Sweet Seed`
  - Average harvest per plot: `8 to 11 Leppa Berries`
  - Re-seeding requirement: Extracting ~2.2 berries yields the 3 seeds needed to replant that plot.
  - **Net Profit per Plot**: `6 to 9 pure profit berries` per plot!
  - Running all 156 Abundant Shrine plots yields **~900 to 1,300 surplus Leppa Berries** every 16 hours!

## 7. Pro Tips for Flawless Harvests

1. **The 8-Hour Rule is Sacred**: Never plant long-cycle crops (especially 67h Lum or 42h EV berries) unless you know you will be available during their 8-hour harvest window.
2. **Plant at Consistent Real-World Hours**: The most reliable time to plant 16h Leppa berries is in the evening (e.g., 10:00 PM). Water before bed, water once in the morning before school/work, and harvest the following afternoon!
3. **Use BerryMaster's Mini-HUD**: If you play PokéMMO on Windows PC, press `Ctrl + H` or tap the HUD icon in BerryMaster to pin the transparent Mini-HUD over your PokéMMO window. You will see live moisture countdowns right next to your game client!
4. **Enable Android Notifications & Haptics**: If you play on Android, ensure BerryMaster has notification permissions enabled so you receive audio/vibrational warnings when your crops need water or ripen.
