# 🌱 PokéMMO Berry Farming: The Complete Beginner's Guide

Welcome to berry farming in PokéMMO! If you're looking for a dependable, semi-passive way to make hundreds of thousands of Pokéyen every single week, you have come to the right place.

This guide will walk you through your very first harvest with zero confusion.

---

## 🧭 Why Farm Berries in PokéMMO?

- **Zero Risk**: Unlike breeding or flipping on the Global Trade Link (GTL), berry farming does not rely on RNG or market speculation.
- **High Demand**: Items like **Leppa Berries** are burned by the tens of thousands daily by shiny hunters, horde EV-trainers, and gym rerun battlers.
- **Semi-Passive**: You only need to log in for 5 to 10 minutes, water or plant your crops, and log off.

---

## 🎒 Step 1: Requirements & Tools

Before you plant your first seed, ensure you have:

### 1. The Watering Tool
You cannot farm without a watering can. Depending on your primary region:
- **Hoenn**: Talk to the girl in the **Pretty Petal Flower Shop** on Route 104 to receive the **Wailmer Pail**.
- **Sinnoh**: Get the **Sprayduck** from the flower shop in **Floaroma Town**.
- **Unova**: Pick up the **Wailmer Pail** in the greenhouse near Route 2 / Accumula Town.

### 2. HM Fly and Surf
Farming requires fast travel between plots and seed extractors:
- Having a Pokémon with **Fly** and **Surf** makes traveling to the 156-plot **Abundant Shrine** in Unova effortless.

### 3. Starting Capital
- **20,000 to 50,000 Pokéyen** to buy your initial batch of starter berries or seeds from the Global Trade Link (GTL).

---

## 🌰 Step 2: How Seeds & Planting Work

In PokéMMO, you **do not plant whole berries directly**. Instead, you plant combinations of **seeds**:

1. Every crop plot requires **exactly 3 seeds** to plant.
2. Berries produce seeds when crushed in a **Seed Extractor** machine (found at Mistralton City in Unova, Route 123 in Hoenn, or Fuego Ironworks in Sinnoh).
3. Extracting 1 berry usually yields **1 or 2 seeds**.

### Seed Flavors:
- **Spicy Seeds** (Red)
- **Dry Seeds** (Blue)
- **Sweet Seeds** (Pink)
- **Bitter Seeds** (Green)
- **Sour Seeds** (Yellow)
- **Plain Seeds** (Gray/White)
- **Very Plain Seeds** (Special neutral seeds)

## 🚀 Step 3: Your First Crop — The "Training Run"

We recommend starting small with **Cheri** or **Pecha** berries before investing millions into Leppas:

### Why Cheri / Pecha?
- They only take **16 hours** to grow.
- Seeds are dirt cheap on the GTL.
- If you miss a watering schedule, your loss is negligible.

### Step-by-Step Planting Walkthrough:
1. **Purchase**: Buy 20 Cheri Berries on the GTL.
2. **Extract**: Take them to the Seed Extractor in **Mistralton City** (Unova). Extract all of them to receive ~28–30 Spicy Seeds.
3. **Travel to Plots**: Head to the nearest plots (e.g., Five Isle Meadow in Kanto or Abundant Shrine in Unova).
4. **Interact with Plot**:
   - Inspect an empty plot.
   - Choose your 3 seeds (for Cheri: `3x Spicy Seeds` or `1x Spicy + 2x Plain`).
   - Confirm planting.
5. **WATER IMMEDIATELY**:
   - Interact with the newly planted seed using your **Wailmer Pail**.
   - Make sure the text says the soil is **"Very Soft"** or **"Dripping"**.
6. **Set Timer in BerryMaster**:
   - Open **BerryMaster**.
   - Tap **Add Character** (or use your existing farmer profile).
   - Tap **Plant Berry** -> select **Cheri Berry**.
   - BerryMaster will automatically track both your **moisture timer** and your **harvest countdown**!

## 💰 Step 4: Upgrading to the Leppa Berry Empire

Once comfortable with the routine, transition 100% of your plots to **Leppa Berries**:

### The Golden Formula:
- **1 Leppa Plot** = `2x Very Plain Spicy Seeds` + `1x Very Plain Sweet Seed`

### The Self-Sustaining Loop:
1. Plant 156 Leppa plots at Abundant Shrine.
2. Water at **Hour 0** (planting) and once around **Hour 8 to 10**.
3. Harvest at **Hour 16**:
   - You will harvest **~1,200 to 1,500 total Leppa Berries**!
4. **Re-seeding (The Secret to Infinite Profit)**:
   - Take ~350–400 berries to the Seed Extractor.
   - Extract them into Spicy and Sweet seeds.
   - You now have all 468 seeds needed to replant the entire 156 plots for **FREE**!
5. **Sell the Rest**:
   - Put the remaining **800 to 1,100 Leppa Berries** on the GTL (selling at ~800 to 950 Pokéyen each).
   - **Net Profit**: **~700,000 to 1,000,000+ Pokéyen every cycle!**

## ⚠️ The 3 Golden Rules — Never Lose a Harvest!

### 1. Beware the 8-Hour Wilt Window
After berries become harvest-ready (e.g., 16 hours for Leppa), you have **exactly 8 hours** to harvest them before they wither away to dust.
- *Rule*: Never plant a crop if you know you will be asleep, at work, or traveling when the harvest window opens!

### 2. Always Water to "Dripping" Right After Planting
Do not walk away from a fresh plot without watering it. Leaving newly planted seeds in dry soil hurts your final yield from minute one.

### 3. Let BerryMaster Handle the Alarms
Don't guess when your crops dry out. Keep BerryMaster open on your PC (`Ctrl + H` Mini-HUD overlay) or phone (haptics and background alerts enabled). You'll always know the exact minute to water and harvest!
